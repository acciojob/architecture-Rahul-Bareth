Architecture

Architecture is the art and science of designing and constructing buildings and other physical structures. It combines creativity, engineering, and functionality to create spaces that are beautiful, safe, and useful.

Importance of Architecture

Architecture plays an important role in our daily lives. It influences how we live, work, and interact with our surroundings. Good architecture focuses on sustainability, comfort, aesthetics, and efficient use of space. Different architectural styles reflect the culture and history of societies.
